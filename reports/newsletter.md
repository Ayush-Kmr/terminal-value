**US Producer Price Index 6.5% Rise: Meaning for Rates**
The U.S. producer price index rose to an annual rate of 6.5% in October, which is raising questions about whether the Federal Reserve will cut interest rates soon. This broadening inflation impacts borrowing costs and the wider economy, affecting what everyday loans and credit might cost you.

**Housing Cliff Changes Mortgage Market**
Housing cliff data reveals a sharp 51% year-over-year drop in mortgage lock demand as consumer borrowing costs remain elevated with the 30-year fixed rate at high levels. This significant slowdown directly impacts the housing market and anyone currently trying to buy or sell a home.

**Target Sales Surge 7.1% On Strong Online Growth**
Target reported that its third-quarter total sales jumped 7.1 percent, marking its best sales growth since 2022, though a one-time government refund supplied a significant part of the adjusted earnings. This gives us a mixed look at how retail shoppers are managing their budgets and where they are spending their money.
