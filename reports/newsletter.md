**Home Depot names a new CEO**
Home Depot named Richard McPhail as its chief executive officer while reporting strong third-quarter financial results. The company adjusted its sales forecast even though it saw fewer customer visits to its stores.
Watch: https://youtu.be/89Yv1aLrXCo

**Credit card giants hit by data breach**
Hackers reached customer names, emails, and some phone numbers held by third-party marketing vendors of Visa, Mastercard, and JPMorgan. All three companies say no card numbers or financial data were taken.
Watch: https://youtu.be/FZ2eHlM7E3Y

**Mortgage demand takes a sharp fall**
Housing cliff data reveals a sharp 51 percent year-over-year drop in mortgage lock demand. Consumer borrowing costs remain elevated with the 30-year fixed rate staying at high levels.
Watch: https://youtu.be/XE5d-pvwH5c
